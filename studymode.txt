Question 1
What is the magnitude of the electric field due to a 6.0 x 10^-9 C charge at a point 0.02m away?
(a) 1.35 x 10^5 N/C
(b) 2.70 x 10^5 N/C
(c) 0.90 x 10^5 N/C
(d) 1.80 x 10^5 N/C
(e) 1.0 x 10^5 N/C

SOLUTION:

k = 9.0 x 10^9 N m²/C²
Q = 6.0 x 10^-9 C
r = 0.02 m
E = (9.0 x 10^9 * 6.0 x 10^-9) / (0.02)^2

ANSWER: (a)
