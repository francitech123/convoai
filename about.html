📖 Our Story

OAU Exam CBT Practice was born from a simple observation: OAU students needed a better way to prepare for CBT exams. As an Electrical/Electronic Engineering student at OAU, the founder, Francis Oluwanje Bobola (FrancisTech), experienced firsthand the challenges of finding quality practice materials for the university's computer-based tests.

What started as a personal project in 2025 has grown into a full-featured platform serving over 10,000 OAU students across all 14 faculties. The platform now houses 45,000+ questions, an AI-powered tutor, a competitive leaderboard system, and a comprehensive course materials repository.

🎯 Our Mission

To democratize access to quality exam preparation for every OAU student — regardless of their financial background. We believe that every student deserves the tools to succeed, which is why we offer a free trial and keep our paid plans affordable (starting at just ₦2,000 per semester).

🚀 What We Offer

Exam Mode

Full CBT simulation with countdown timer, random question selection, and instant grading — identical to the real OAU exam experience.

Test Mode

Self-paced practice with helpful hints and detailed explanations for every answer. Learn as you go.

AI Tutor (New!)

Our intelligent AI assistant provides instant explanations, study tips, and answers to your questions 24/7. Powered by advanced language models.

Leaderboard

Compete with fellow students, track your ranking, and stay motivated with streaks and achievements.

Analytics Dashboard

Track your progress with detailed stats — exams taken, average scores, study time, streak history, and performance trends.

Course Materials

Access downloadable slides, past questions, textbooks, and video tutorials uploaded by administrators and fellow students.

Flexible Payment Plans

Choose from Free Trial, Semester Plan (₦2,000), or Full Session Plan (₦3,500). Secure payment processing with instant access.

Admin & CEO Panel

Volunteer administrators help maintain question quality. The CEO panel manages approvals, analytics, and platform governance.

💻 Technology Stack

The platform is built with modern, scalable technologies:

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript — fast, lightweight, and works on all devices
  • Backend: Node.js with Express.js — high-performance API server
  • Database: MongoDB — flexible document database for questions, users, and analytics
  • AI Integration: Custom AI chat system for instant tutoring and explanations
  • Hosting: Render (backend) and Vercel (frontend) — reliable, fast, and globally distributed
  • Payment Processing: Secure payment gateway integration for plan purchases

💰 Our Pricing Philosophy

We believe quality exam preparation should be accessible to everyone. Our pricing is designed to cover operational costs (servers, domain, AI services) while keeping it affordable for students:

  • Free Trial: Always available so you can test the platform before committing
  • ₦2,000/Semester: Less than the cost of one textbook — for unlimited access to all courses
  • ₦3,500/Full Session: Best value — covers both semesters with extra benefits

We also offer a referral program where you can earn rewards by inviting friends to the platform.

🔒 Security & Privacy

We take your data seriously:

  • All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing
  • Payment information is processed through secure payment gateways
  • Personal data is never shared with third parties
  • Account recovery is protected by security questions
  • Administrators are vetted through an application process
