2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Platform Functionality: To create and manage your account, authenticate logins, process payments, and provide access to features based on your plan.
  • Personalization: To display your name, profile picture, faculty, level, and personalized dashboard stats.
  • Leaderboard & Analytics: To calculate rankings, display top performers, and provide performance analytics.
  • AI Tutor: To process your questions and provide AI-generated responses. Chat interactions may be used to improve the AI service.
  • Notifications: To send platform announcements, CEO messages, admin notifications, and study reminders based on your preferences.
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse of the Platform.
  • Communication: To respond to your chat messages, support requests, and admin applications.
  • Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve the Platform's features and performance.

3. AI Integration & Data Processing

3.1 AI Tutor

Our AI Tutor feature processes your questions and returns intelligent responses. When you interact with the AI Tutor:

  • Your question text is sent to our AI service for processing
  • Your course context (faculty, level) may be used to provide more relevant answers
  • AI conversations are not shared with other users
  • Aggregated, anonymized interaction data may be used to improve the AI service
  • Personal identifiers are stripped before any data is used for AI training

3.2 AI Limitations

⚠️ Important: The AI Tutor is a study aid, not a guaranteed source of truth. Always verify critical academic information with your course materials, textbooks, and lecturers. We are not responsible for decisions made based solely on AI-generated responses.

4. Payment Information

All payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways. We do not store your full credit card, debit card, or bank account details on our servers. The payment gateway provides us with:

  • Transaction confirmation (success/failure)
  • Transaction reference number
  • Payment plan selected
  • Amount paid and date

4.2 Payment Records

We retain payment transaction records for:

  • Account management: To verify your access level and plan status
  • Dispute resolution: To handle refund requests and payment disputes
  • Legal compliance: As required by applicable laws and regulations

6. Data Storage & Security

6.1 Storage Location

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Render (backend) and MongoDB Atlas (database). These providers employ industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit.

6.2 Security Measures

  • Password Encryption: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt with 12 rounds of salting
  • HTTPS: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Authentication: JWT (JSON Web Tokens) are used for session management
  • Rate Limiting: API endpoints are protected against brute-force attacks
  • Helmet.js: HTTP security headers are enforced
  • CORS Protection: Only authorized domains can access the API
  • Input Validation: All user inputs are validated and sanitized

7. Your Rights & Choices

7.1 Access & Correction

You can access and update your personal information through the Profile page on the Platform. This includes your full name, email, faculty, department, level, profile picture, and preferences.

7.2 Data Portability

Upon request, we can provide you with a copy of your personal data in a machine-readable format. Contact us through the Chat feature to request data export.

7.3 Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account through the Profile page. This will:

  • Remove all personal information from our active database
  • Delete your exam history, scores, achievements, and preferences
  • Remove your notifications and chat messages
  • Remove your comments and reviews
  • Retain anonymized statistical data for platform analytics

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. We cannot recover deleted accounts or their associated data.

7.4 Notification Preferences

You can manage your notification preferences (dark mode, email notifications, push notifications, study reminders) through the Preferences section on your Profile page.

7.5 Cookies

The Platform uses localStorage and sessionStorage (browser storage) to maintain your login session and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can clear this data through your browser settings at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for university-level students (typically age 16 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.
